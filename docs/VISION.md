# MatchFlix Vision

## The Long-Term Direction

MatchFlix transforms how groups make entertainment decisions together. Today, that starts with movies. Tomorrow, it extends to TV shows, games, restaurants, activities—any decision that involves multiple people with different tastes.

The core insight is simple: decision fatigue kills enjoyment. People would rather watch a movie they didn't pick than spend an hour picking one. MatchFlix removes that friction by making group decisions instant and painless.

## What Success Looks Like

When a group of friends says "let's watch something," they think of MatchFlix before opening Netflix. When a couple wants to try a new restaurant, they use MatchFlix to find one they both like. When a family picks a game night activity, MatchFlix is the decision engine.

The product becomes synonymous with group decision-making, not movie recommendations.

## Principles

**Playful, not serious.** The experience is fun and interactive, not technical or heavy.

**Frictionless.** No accounts, no profiles, no history. Just a quick quiz and an instant answer.

**Preference-based, not algorithmic.** MatchFlix recommends based on what people say they like, not on watch history or learned behavior.

**Always shows streaming info.** A recommendation is useless if you don't know where to watch it.

**Built for groups.** Every feature assumes multiple people with different opinions arriving at one decision.

## Future Scope (Out of MVP)

TV shows with series length and episode count considerations. Games matching across genres and player count. Restaurants integrating with reservation systems. Activities (concerts, museums, hikes) with location and availability. Explained recommendations showing what everyone agreed on. Preference persistence across sessions for returning users.
