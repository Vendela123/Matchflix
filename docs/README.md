# MatchFlix

Find a movie everyone wants to watch in seconds, not hours.

## What is MatchFlix?

Choosing a movie together has become harder than watching the movie itself. With thousands of titles spread across multiple streaming services, groups waste time deciding instead of enjoying their evening.

MatchFlix solves this instantly. Each person answers a short preference quiz—genres, mood, runtime—and the platform combines everyone's tastes to recommend a movie they'll all enjoy. No accounts, no sign-ups, no friction. Just answers a quiz, get a recommendation, and hit play.

## Who is it for?

Couples, friends, and families who want to stop scrolling and start watching.

## How it works

1. One person creates a match session and shares the link
2. Everyone joins and answers a quick preference quiz
3. The platform instantly recommends the best movie for the group
4. See what to watch, where to watch it, and start playing

## The problem it solves

Decision fatigue kills the evening before the movie even starts. MatchFlix removes the negotiation and the endless browsing by turning individual preferences into a single, shared recommendation.

## Vision

MatchFlix becomes the go-to platform for helping groups make entertainment decisions effortlessly—movies today, TV shows, games, restaurants, and activities tomorrow.
